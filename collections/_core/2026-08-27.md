## PR Slop

- Please, for the love of god, can we stop having slop PR, especially where reading the entire thing takes more time than the lifetime integrated time saved by the actions in the PR.
- If you can't be bothered to write it, I don't see why anyone should be expected to read it or review it or spend any time thinking about it (except you are forcing people to think about it by filing the PR).
- Especially when it's entirely unclear without wading through the whole thing if there is actually any benefit from the PR.
